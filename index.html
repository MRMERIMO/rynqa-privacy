RYNQA Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026. This policy covers the RYNQA Android app (com.reko.rynqa) and its support contact, rynqa.support@gmail.com.

RYNQA helps you understand supported Dart and Flutter source projects. Analysis runs on your device. The app does not upload your source, saved analysis, Graph or Findings to an analysis service. No RYNQA account is required. This release has no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases or automatic analytics uploads.

Source files you select

You choose a ZIP archive or folder through Android's file picker and confirm you are authorized to analyze it. RYNQA reads the selected source to validate the project, create its graph and produce supported findings. It does not execute imported project code or modify your original files.

When local copying is supported, ZIP import makes a temporary private copy of the whole archive, including entries later excluded from analysis. Only approved project files are analyzed. Folder import copies the selected project files into private storage. If local copying is unavailable, the supported direct-source import path is used. Keep the source unchanged while copying; later edits do not automatically update a saved analysis.

Saved projects, retention and deletion

Project records and analysis can contain file paths, symbol names, relationships and finding evidence. Saved results and preferences stay in the app's private storage until you remove them, clear the app's data or uninstall it. Reopening original source requires user selection and verification; a saved result does not itself grant continuing access to your original source.

Use Remove project to delete a saved project's record and analysis. This does not delete the original source files you selected. Temporary source copies are cleaned up when the app releases them; interrupted work may be cleaned up during recovery. Project removal and ordinary file deletion are not forensic secure erasure.

Private results, preferences and temporary source copies are excluded from supported Android backup and device-transfer mechanisms. Reinstallation may therefore lose saved results. This cannot prevent every rooted-device, manual-copy or operating-system access scenario.

Diagnostics and clipboard

While the production app is running, it automatically maintains limited counts of predefined error categories in memory for the current session. The Safe Diagnostics screen shows these counts. They do not contain source contents, project names, file paths, device identifiers, exception messages or stack traces. The app may also write a fixed error-category label to the device's system log; it does not include the underlying error or source text. These diagnostics are not automatically uploaded. Copying a report is optional and happens only when you choose the copy action.

When you choose a copy action, RYNQA places the requested content on the device clipboard. Depending on the action, this can include a source-file path, selected source text, a whole source file, the support email address or a diagnostics report. Copying does not itself upload that content. Clipboard access, clipboard synchronization where enabled, and any later pasting or sharing are controlled by Android and the other apps or services you use.

Developer Graph recorders and temporary engineering measurement tools are excluded from the production app. There is no hidden remote activation or background performance-report upload.

Permissions and other apps

RYNQA uses Android's file picker for selected access, rather than requesting unrestricted access to your storage. Its vibration permission supports optional touch feedback. The production app does not request internet, camera, microphone, contacts or location permissions.

Share RYNQA opens Android's share interface with fixed app information. Write email opens your email app addressed to support. RYNQA does not send the email itself, and does not automatically attach your project, diagnostics or files. Opening another app does not give RYNQA control over that app's data handling.

Contacting support

Email rynqa.support@gmail.com for help, privacy questions or a request to delete your support correspondence. When you choose to email, RYNQA support and the email providers receive your address and whatever you send. Do not include confidential source code, passwords, private keys or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is used to answer your request and resolve related issues. It is retained only as needed for those purposes and any applicable legal obligations. You may request its deletion at the same address; verification may be needed to protect other people's correspondence. Email-provider retention and backup practices also apply. This is separate from project data stored only on your device, which support cannot access or delete remotely.

This policy page

This page contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels or forms that upload project files. The web-hosting provider may process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and browser request information, to serve and protect the page under its own privacy policy. Visiting a policy website is separate from the app's offline analysis.

Changes

If RYNQA's data handling changes, this policy and the relevant in-app and store disclosures will be updated. The date above identifies this revision. Contact rynqa.support@gmail.com with any questions.
